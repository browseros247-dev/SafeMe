Troubleshooting

Why it matters
Some phones stop apps from running in the background to save battery. If SafeMe ever stops blocking, this is usually why. SafeMe never requests autostart itself — here's how to turn it on for your phone.
1
Open the Security app
Tap the Security app from your home screen, or search for it in Settings.
2
Go to Permissions → Autostart
On HyperOS it lives under Security → Permissions.
3
Switch SafeMe on
Find SafeMe in the list and enable its switch — that's it.
1
Settings → Apps → App management
Scroll to SafeMe and tap it.
2
Open Permissions
Tap Permissions inside the app page.
3
Enable Allow auto-launch
On some versions it's under Battery → Allow background activity instead.
1
Settings → Applications → Autostart
Open the Autostart manager.
2
Find SafeMe
It's listed among apps that can auto-start.
3
Flip the switch on
Also check Settings → Battery → Background power consumption → Allow.
1
Settings → Apps → SafeMe
Open the app's settings page.
2
Tap Battery
Under the app's permissions list.
3
Choose Unrestricted
Samsung has no separate autostart toggle — unrestricted battery is the fix.
1
Settings → Apps → SafeMe
Open the app's settings page.
2
Tap Battery → Unrestricted
Stock Android has no autostart manager; unrestricted battery keeps SafeMe alive.
3
Done
If notifications still stop, also check SafeMe's notification settings.
